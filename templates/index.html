How it works

  1. List your classes. We use them to show you parties and classmates for CHEM 1315, CS 2413, or whatever you're taking.
  2. Join a party or start a session. A party is a study group meeting at a campus spot. You can also study solo.
  3. Hit Finish when you're done. You get 1 Crimson Crystal and 2 XP per minute, and 50% more when you study with a party.
  4. Spend and compete. Buy avatar items, climb the leaderboards, and add your hours to your club, fraternity, or sorority's clan.
